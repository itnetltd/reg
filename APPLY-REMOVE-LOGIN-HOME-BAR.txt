REG public header cleanup

This hotfix:
- removes the Drupal Home / Log in / My account / Log out strip from the public theme;
- prevents account-menu blocks from being rendered in the REG header;
- keeps the REG logo, primary navigation, search and Figma-style utility links;
- keeps /user/login available for administrators by direct URL.

Apply from Windows CMD:

  cd /d C:\Sites\reg-drupal
  powershell -NoProfile -Command "Expand-Archive -Path '%USERPROFILE%\Downloads\reg-remove-login-home-bar-hotfix.zip' -DestinationPath 'C:\Sites\reg-drupal' -Force"
  ddev drush cr

Then refresh the browser with Ctrl+Shift+R.
