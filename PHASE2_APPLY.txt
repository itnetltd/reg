REG Drupal Phase Two Update
===========================

1. Extract this ZIP over the root of C:\Sites\reg-drupal.
2. Open Command Prompt in C:\Sites\reg-drupal.
3. Run:

   ddev exec bash ./scripts/apply-phase2.sh

4. Open:

   ddev launch

5. Configure approved links and calculator assumptions at:

   /admin/config/reg/site-settings

The update creates structured content types, editorial roles, multilingual readiness,
Online Services gateway, Outage Center, FAQ-first assistant, Bill Estimator, Carbon
Calculator, and a service-first homepage aligned to the revised inception report.
