REG logo + FAQ floating action hotfix

1. Extract this ZIP into C:\Sites\reg-drupal and allow overwrite.
2. From CMD run:

   cd /d C:\Sites\reg-drupal
   ddev drush cr

3. Reload the browser with Ctrl+Shift+R.
